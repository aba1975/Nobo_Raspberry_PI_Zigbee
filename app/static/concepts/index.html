Three UI design concepts

Three genuinely different ways to organise the same heating system. Each one is a working prototype running against your real zones and your real hub — not a mockup. Change a temperature in any of them and it changes for real.

These are prototypes for you to compare. They live on the branch ui-redesign-exploration, they do not replace the current app, and nothing has been merged. The current app is untouched at the usual address. Each concept covers everyday control; hub setup, users, device management, weekly schedule editing and the command log stay in the current app.
Concept A

At a Glance

The whole house on one screen as a grid of room tiles. Built to answer “which rooms are cold?” in one look, then let you drop into a room to adjust it.

  • Room tiles with the measured temperature at display size
  • Device picture built into every tile
  • Temperature set with a slider in a side drawer
  • Comparison first, control second

Concept B

Room First

The app behaves like the thermostat itself. One room fills the screen, the hardware is shown as a real object, and the target is set on a dial.

  • Rooms switched from a rail, no page hierarchy at all
  • Largest, clearest presentation of the device images
  • Temperature set on a draggable dial
  • Best single-room experience, weakest overview
Open concept B
Concept C

Heating Board

One surface, no navigation. Every room is a row you can read and adjust where it sits; opening a room expands it in place or fills a second pane on a wide screen.

  • Change a temperature without opening anything
  • Device thumbnails inline in every row
  • Sort by coldest, filter to what is heating
  • Scales best to a house with many zones
Open concept C

How to judge them

Try each one twice: once on a laptop and once on your phone. Ask yourself the same four questions each time — can I tell what the heating is doing within a couple of seconds, can I find the cold room, can I raise a room by three degrees without thinking, and do I know which physical device I am controlling?
